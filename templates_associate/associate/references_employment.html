    Employment History Requirements

    Please read the following carefully before completing this page.

    Coverage Requirement
    • You must provide employment reference details covering the full 5 years prior to today's date.
    • All employment periods within this window must be listed, including part-time, contract, temporary, and agency work.
    • The entire 5-year period must be accounted for with no unexplained gaps.
    Employment Gaps
    • Any gap in employment exceeding 90 days must be declared and explained.
    • Supporting evidence is mandatory for each qualifying gap (e.g. P45, medical certificate, bank statement, official correspondence).
    • Evidence should be emailed to: compliance@optimussolutions.co.uk
    HMRC Employment Record

    We will need your HMRC employment record to complete the vetting process. Your HMRC employment record will show employer records including dates and PAYE references, and so this might also help you complete your employment timeline.

    Access your HMRC employment record here: www.gov.uk/get-proof-employment-history. Once downloaded, upload the form using the panel below.

    Acceptable evidence by gap type:
    • Unemployment: Job Seekers' Allowance / Universal Credit statements, bank statements showing incoming payments

    • Other: Payslips, P60/P45, employment contract, personal or professional reference
