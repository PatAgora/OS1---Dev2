Expenses Categories

Configure the categories Associates pick from when adding expense lines, and their VAT treatment (Standard 20% / Reduced 5% / Zero 0% / Non-VATable). Default for new categories is Standard 20%. Mileage is non-VATable and uses the HMRC rate.

{# Default VAT rate — central config, fallback for every invoice line item #}
Default VAT Rate

Used as the fallback VAT rate on every invoice line item when no explicit rate is supplied. Changes apply immediately to new invoices — historical invoices keep their persisted rate.

{# PDF retention — central config, drives the nightly cleanup cron #}
Invoice PDF Retention

How long generated + sent invoice PDFs are kept in the document store. The nightly cleanup cron deletes any PDF older than this. Default is 2555 days (7 years) — adjust per your tax-record retention policy.

{# HMRC mileage rate — central config, used by every Mileage-flagged category #}
HMRC Mileage Rate

Mileage-flagged categories compute reimbursable amount as distance × this rate. Adjust here when HMRC publishes a new rate.
